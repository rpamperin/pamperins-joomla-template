Pamperins Tech is a small, locally owned IT business in Northeast Wisconsin. This page explains, in plain words, what information we collect, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated: January 1, 2027

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to help you.
  • We never sell your information, and we don't show ads.
  • Remote sessions only start when you click Allow, and you can end them at any time.
  • Session recordings are stored encrypted and deleted after 90 days.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete your information.

What we collect

When you create an account

Your name, e-mail address and username, and your password. We never see or store your password itself; it's stored in a scrambled (hashed) form.

When you ask for help (support tickets)

What you type into the request form: the problem, your answers about your computer, network or project, and, if you give them, your phone number, address (for on-site visits) and the best way to reach you. We also keep our replies and notes about the work.

When we help you remotely

Remote help runs on our own support system at support.pamperins.com. Before connecting you see and accept our support agreement, and nothing happens until you click Allow. While a session is running we record what we do, our chat, and pictures of your screen while it is shared. Recordings are stored encrypted and deleted after 90 days. We only look at them to check our work or to sort out a problem you report.

When you contact us

Messages you send through our contact form, by e-mail or by phone.

Cookies

  • To keep a history of the work we've done for you.

  • For billing, if the work is paid. [CONFIRM]
